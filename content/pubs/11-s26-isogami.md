## Overview

IsoGami explores rigid-deployable kirigami sheets built from isohedral tilings. The method searches joint topologies and tile shapes, identifies designs with a single deployment degree of freedom, and computes collision-aware unfolding motions. An interactive material browser and 3D-printed prototypes demonstrate the resulting designs.
